**App name:** BenthicCam

**Short description** (80 characters max):
Geotag GoPro survey photos with your phone's surface GPS.

**Full description:**

BenthicCam makes benthic photo surveys faster and better organised. Control your GoPro from a phone or tablet at the surface, take each quadrat photo at the right moment, and have the surface GPS position written straight into the photo.

HOW IT WORKS
The GoPro goes down on a pole, frame or drop line while you stay topside with the phone. Press the shutter in the app, or use the live view to time it. BenthicCam records the phone's GPS position at the moment the shutter fires, downloads the photo from the camera over Wi-Fi, and saves it with the position, time and height embedded as standard EXIF data. You no longer need to match GPS logs to photo times afterwards.

BUILT FOR SURVEY WORK
• Survey camera settings: compares the camera's current settings with the recommended survey settings (Linear lens, RAW, 1/250 s shutter, EV −0.5, ISO 100–400, controlled white balance and sharpness) and applies them in one tap
• Live view with a shutter button for framing the quadrat
• Review each photo full screen, then keep it or retake it
• Folders for sites, transects or dives
• GPS track recording, exported as a KMZ for Google Earth with a thumbnail at every photo
• Satellite map that shows GPS accuracy, so you know the fix is good before you shoot
• Sets the camera clock from the phone so the camera's own timestamps match
• Works offline in the field: map areas you've viewed beforehand stay cached

REQUIREMENTS
GoPro HERO9 Black or newer. Android phone or tablet with Bluetooth and GPS.

A NOTE ON POSITION
The embedded position is the phone's location at the surface. Allow for any horizontal offset between the phone and the camera, such as the pole angle or drift on a drop line.
